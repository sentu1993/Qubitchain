# Qubitchain.io Roadmap

The Qubitchain roadmap is strictly aligned with the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Post-Quantum Cryptography finalization timelines, the migration guidelines set by regulatory bodies (DORA, NIS2, SEC), and the evolving quantum hardware threat landscape.

## Phase 1: Cryptographic Foundation (2024 - 2025)
**Status: COMPLETED**

* **NIST Standardization Alignment:** Following the August 2024 release of FIPS 203, 204, and 205, Qubitchain finalized its core cryptographic library selections.
* **ML-DSA & ML-KEM Prototyping:** Successful integration of CRYSTALS-Dilithium for transaction signing and CRYSTALS-Kyber for node communication in isolated test environments.
* **QRNG Hardware Sourcing:** Established partnerships for certified Quantum Random Number Generator hardware integration.

## Phase 2: Consensus & Testnet Alpha (Early 2026)
**Status: COMPLETED**

* **Proof of Quantum Entropy (PoQE) Development:** Finalized the mathematics and architecture for QRNG-driven validator selection.
* **Internal Alpha Testnet:** Deployed the first natively quantum-safe network operating at 1,000+ TPS using lattice-based signatures.
* **Whitepaper Publication:** Release of the comprehensive Qubitchain technical vision.

## Phase 3: Waitlist, Community & Public Testnet (Mid 2026 - Current)
**Status: IN PROGRESS**

* **Global Awareness Campaign:** Educating the crypto ecosystem on the "Harvest Now, Decrypt Later" threat and the rapid advancement of neutral-atom quantum computers.
* **Waitlist Registration:** Opening early access for validators, delegators, and the Community Token Generation Event (TGE).
* **Public Testnet (Quantum-0):** Opening the network to external node operators to stress-test ML-DSA signature verification times and PoQE consensus.

## Phase 4: Mainnet Genesis & TGE (Late 2026)
**Status: UPCOMING**

* **Security Audits:** Multiple independent third-party audits of our post-quantum cryptographic implementations and smart contract VM.
* **Token Generation Event (TGE):** Distribution of the QBIT token according to the established tokenomics structure.
* **Mainnet Launch:** The genesis block is minted, establishing the world's first natively quantum-safe Layer 1 blockchain.

## Phase 5: Cryptographic Agility & Scaling (2027+)
**Status: PLANNED**

* **FIPS 206 Integration:** Anticipating the finalization of the Falcon signature scheme (FN-DSA), Qubitchain will activate its cryptographic agility framework to support multi-algorithm verification.
* **Layer 2 Quantum Integration:** Extending quantum-safe state channels and rollups to scale throughput exponentially while maintaining base-layer post-quantum security.
* **Enterprise Compliance Hub:** Deploying enterprise-grade infrastructure to support financial institutions migrating under DORA, NIS2, and SEC guidelines.
